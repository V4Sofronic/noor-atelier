# Important note about the photos

The photos and frames in this site were collected as screenshots from other websites, brand pages,
photographers and videos, for the purpose of previewing how the store looks. Many belong to other people
and brands, and some still show logos or watermarks (for example, a bridal studio, a photographer, and
fabric brands). Several show children and real models.

**This was built as a private preview.** Do not publish the site publicly, or sell clothes through it,
using these images unless you have permission. Publishing them could lead to copyright claims, takedowns,
legal trouble, and misleading customers (they would expect to receive the exact outfits shown).

## Before going live

- Replace the photos with your own product photos, photos from suppliers or brands who have given you
  written permission (WhatsApp or email is fine), or properly licensed stock photos.
- Do not use photos of children without permission from the photographers and the children's parents.
- Replace the animation frames (`images/hero` and `images/party-maxi`) with your own video frames or
  licensed footage.
- Replace placeholder product names and prices with your real ones.

Replacing a photo is easy: see section 3 of `EDITING-GUIDE.md`.
